Day 1: kitchen tour, locate the fire extinguishers, first-aid kit, eyewash station, and the handwashing sinks. Get issued two aprons, a side towel, and a probe thermometer.

Day 2: knife-safety training with the sous chef. Every new cook must be signed off on knife safety before working a station alone.

Day 3: food-safety basics covering handwashing, cold holding at 41F or below, cross-contact, and the allergen list. Read the Allergen Cross-Contact Prevention SOP.

Day 4 and 5: shadow a Prep cook, then a Grill cook. By Friday you should be able to set up a station using its checklist.

Ask questions early. Nobody expects a new cook to know where everything is.
