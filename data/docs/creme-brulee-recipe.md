Yield: 12 portions in 6 oz ramekins.

Ingredients: 1 quart heavy cream, 1 vanilla bean (split and scraped), 8 egg yolks, 3/4 cup sugar plus extra for the crust.

Method: heat the cream with the vanilla bean until steaming, then steep for 15 minutes. Whisk the yolks and sugar until pale. Temper the warm cream into the yolks slowly, strain, and pour into ramekins.

Bake in a water bath at 300F for 35 to 40 minutes, until the edges are set and the center still wobbles slightly. Chill for at least 4 hours before service.

To finish: sprinkle a thin layer of sugar on each custard and torch until deeply caramelized. Serve within 5 minutes so the crust stays crisp.
