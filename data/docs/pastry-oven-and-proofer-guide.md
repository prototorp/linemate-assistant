Oven calibration: check the deck oven against an oven-safe thermometer at the start of each week. If the thermometer differs from the dial by more than 15F, stop baking on that deck and open a ticket.

Preheat: the deck oven needs 45 minutes to reach 425F. Load bread first, then pastry at lower temperatures as the oven cools during the day.

Proofer settings: hold the proofer at 80F and 75 percent humidity for yeast doughs. Cover dough trays with a damp cloth if humidity drops below 70 percent.

Cleaning: brush the oven decks after every bake and wipe the proofer gasket at close.
