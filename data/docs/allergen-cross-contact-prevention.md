The nine major allergens tracked at Hearthline are milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, and sesame.

Allergy orders: any ticket flagged ALLERGY is prepared at a cleaned station, with a fresh purple-handled knife, purple cutting board, and clean gloves. Wash hands and change gloves before touching the plate.

Labeling: every prep container in the walk-in carries a label with the item name, prep date, use-by date, and allergen list. Unlabeled containers are discarded at close.

Shared fryer rule: fries are never cooked in the same oil as breaded or battered items for an allergy order. Use the dedicated allergy fryer basket or cook the item in a clean pan.

If cross-contact is suspected, stop service on that item, tell the sous chef, and remake the dish from scratch.
