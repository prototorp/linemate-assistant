Purpose: every steak leaves the grill at the doneness the guest ordered.

Pull temperatures (measured at the thickest point with a calibrated probe): rare 120F, medium-rare 130F, medium 140F, medium-well 150F, well done 160F. Carryover cooking adds roughly 5F during the rest, so pull 5F below the target.

Rest times: rest every steak for 5 minutes on the warming rack before plating; thick cuts over 2 inches rest for 8 minutes. Never slice or plate a steak before its rest is complete.

Ticket timing: call "fire" on steaks only when the appetizers hit the pass. A steak that has rested longer than 10 minutes must be remade rather than served.

Poultry is never cooked to order: chicken always reaches 165F internal temperature.
