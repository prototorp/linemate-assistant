---
Scope: all three fryers on the Grill line.

Filtration: filter each fryer twice per day, once at mid-shift and once at close. Oil must be below 150F before filtering. Wear heat-resistant gloves and the face shield, and never filter a fryer while another cook is working at it.

Oil changes: change the oil in a fryer every 4 days, or sooner if the oil darkens, foams, smells off, or smokes below 350F. Record every change on the fryer log posted beside the unit.

Disposal: pour cooled used oil into the sealed disposal drum in the back alley. Never pour oil down a drain. The rendering company collects the drum every Tuesday.

Fryer temperature: hold fryers at 350F for fries and 375F for chicken. If a fryer cannot hold temperature, take it out of service and open a ticket.
