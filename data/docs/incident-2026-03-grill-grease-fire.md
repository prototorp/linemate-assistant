Date: March 14, 2026, during Friday lunch service.

What happened: grease built up in the hood above the flat-top ignited. The line cook used the Class K extinguisher and the fire was out in under a minute. No one was injured.

Impact: the grill line was closed for 25 minutes and the hood filters were replaced.

Root cause: the hood filters had missed their weekly cleaning.

Corrective actions: hood filters are now cleaned every week and logged, and every Grill cook must locate the Class K extinguisher during onboarding.
