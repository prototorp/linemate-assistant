Cold holding: all TCS (time/temperature control for safety) food in the walk-in must be held at 41F or below. The walk-in air temperature target is 36F to 38F.

Temperature log: the opening Prep cook records the walk-in temperature at open, mid-shift, and close on the clipboard by the door. Initial every entry. A missing entry is a food-safety finding.

If the walk-in reads above 41F: recheck with a second thermometer after 15 minutes. If it is still above 41F, move TCS food to the reach-in coolers, tell the sous chef immediately, and open a Critical ticket. Food that has been above 41F for more than 4 hours must be discarded.

Storage order (top to bottom): ready-to-eat food, seafood, whole cuts of beef and pork, ground meat, poultry. Raw poultry is always on the bottom shelf.
