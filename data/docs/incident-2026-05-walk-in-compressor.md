Date: May 3, 2026, during Saturday dinner service.

What happened: the walk-in compressor failed at about 6 PM. The cooler climbed from 37F to 46F over three hours before the missing log entries were noticed by the closing cook.

Impact: about 90 pounds of chicken, dairy, and prepared sauces were discarded because they had been above 41F for more than 4 hours. Two menu items were 86ed on Sunday.

Root cause: the temperature log was not filled in at mid-shift, so the rise went unnoticed.

Corrective actions: added a mid-shift log entry to the Walk-in Cold-Holding SOP and installed a door alarm. The refrigeration contractor replaced the compressor on May 5.
