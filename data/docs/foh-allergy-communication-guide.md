Ask every table whether anyone has a food allergy before taking the first order.

When a guest reports an allergy: enter it in the POS with the ALLERGY flag, name the specific allergen, and walk the ticket to the kitchen instead of only sending it through the printer.

The expo confirms the allergy plate personally and hands it to the server, who delivers it directly to the guest. Never let a runner deliver an allergy plate.

If a guest asks about ingredients you cannot answer, do not guess. Check with the sous chef.
